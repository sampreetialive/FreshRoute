MVP METHODOLOGY

Planning pipeline

From input data to a distribution recommendation

01
Inventory & destination data

Batch quantity, produce type, remaining shelf life, selling price, destination demand, transit time, transport cost and capacity.

02
Feasibility screening

Check stock availability, demand ceilings, destination capacity and whether handling plus transit fits the remaining shelf life.

03
Priority score

Rank feasible options using urgency, contribution after variable costs and demand fit. The scoring heuristic is transparent but not globally optimal.

04
Baseline & evaluation

Run a simple input-order allocation under the same physical constraints, then compare fulfilment, estimated spoilage and net revenue.

05
Actionable output

Show the recommended allocations, unfilled demand, stock left over, discount rules and reasons for key decisions.

Core assumptions

  • Shelf life is a provided estimate and is not a food-safety guarantee.
  • Demand and market prices are simulated and static until edited.
  • Revenue is computed only for allocated quantities under the demo model.
  • Transport and handling costs are applied consistently across strategies.
  • Estimated spoilage is simplified to unallocated quantity that expires within the planning horizon.
  • Results depend on the dataset and heuristic; they do not establish real-world performance.
